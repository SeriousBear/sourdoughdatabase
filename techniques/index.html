Techniques

The mechanics, explained.

Recipes tell you to stretch and fold, autolyse, pre-shape, score. Very few tell you what any of it is for. These pages do — one technique at a time, with what it does, when it matters, and when you can skip it.

home/ techniques

Every recipe on this site links out to these instead of re-explaining the same moves in every post. The advantage of separating them is that you can learn a technique once, properly, in the context of what it's physically doing to the dough — and then recognise it everywhere. The library is complete — nine techniques covering the whole process, from the first rest through the bake. If something has already gone wrong, the Trouble Atlas works backwards from the symptom instead.
