Bake Schedule Builder.

Tell it when you want fresh bread. It tells you everything else — when to feed your starter, when bulk finishes, when to shape, when to sleep. Adjusts in real time as your kitchen temperature changes.

What these timings assume Temperature is the biggest lever, and it always was. Your flour blend and how much starter you use are the next two, and this page now asks for both — set them below and every timing moves with them. The same engine runs Choose Your Own Crumb, so the two tools agree on the same dough.

What it still cannot know is your exact formula. Hydration is assumed around 70%, and fat, sugar, dairy and acid all slow a dough down without appearing here — if you're baking something enriched or sour, build it properly and read the timings off that instead.

Either way, treat these as a starting estimate, not a stopwatch. The dough decides — reading the bulk covers the four signals that actually tell you when it's done.
