The Library

Recipes, explained.

Every recipe here tells you why, not just what. Weights, timings, and the reasoning behind both — cross-referenced to the calculators and the Trouble Atlas, so when something goes sideways you know exactly where to look.

home/ recipes

Two kinds of recipe live here. Ours are written from scratch in this kitchen, with the reasoning and the arguments left in. Adapted ones take a formula that's already proven itself — the ingredient weights, which are facts and belong to everyone — and rebuild the method in our own words, with full credit and a link back to the baker who got there first. We never reprint anyone's writing. If you want their version, and you should, the link is right there at the top of the page.

Recipes here link out to Techniques rather than re-explaining folds and shaping every time, and to Starter School if you don't have a culture yet.
